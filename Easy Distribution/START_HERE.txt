SOMBIE CLAN COMPANION
PORTABLE WINDOWS VERSION

WELCOME
This folder contains the complete app and the files it needs to run.

TO START
1. Extract the ZIP to a normal folder on your computer.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click "Start Sombie Companion.bat" or "SombieCompanion.exe".

You do NOT need to install LÖVE, Lua, or any other runtime separately.
The required runtime files are already included.

IMPORTANT
Keep SombieCompanion.exe and the DLL files together in this folder.
Do not try to run the app from inside the ZIP. Extract the ZIP first.

YOUR DATA
Your clan records and attachments are saved locally on your computer.
They are not automatically shared with anyone else and are not sent to a server.
