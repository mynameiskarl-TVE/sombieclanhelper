SOMBIE CLAN COMPANION
WINDOWS SETUP

1. Extract this ZIP to a normal folder.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click "Install Sombie Companion.bat".
4. Press ENTER when the setup window asks.

The installer copies the app and its required runtime files to your local
Programs folder, creates a desktop shortcut, and starts the app.

No administrator permission, LÖVE installation, Lua installation, or internet
connection is required on the computer receiving this ZIP.

IMPORTANT
- Extract the ZIP before running the installer.
- Keep the installed app files together.
- Your clan records and copied attachments stay on this computer.
- The app does not sync data with other players or send it to a server.

To launch the app again, double-click the "Sombie Clan Companion" desktop icon.
